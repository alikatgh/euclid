# Curriculum and source map

This curriculum contains 144 original companion lessons. It follows the instructional topics of Volume 1, third edition, while retaining the source as the place to read the full original development. The diagrams and transfer questions are newly authored.

## Using a lesson

Read the technical passage first or select it to see a worked state. Play or step through the causal transformation. Use **I still don’t understand** for a separately computed smaller example in the same panel. Answer the transfer question, including the reason. Return to the source section and try to read its notation without relying on the example.

Source PDF locators in individual lessons count pages of the supplied reflowed ebook starting at one; they are not printed page numbers. Section identifiers are the stable cross-edition navigation aid.

## Coverage boundaries

- Chapter 1: algorithms, mathematical preliminaries, MIX, and fundamental programming techniques.
- Chapter 2: linear lists, trees, multilinked structures, storage allocation, and the instructional pointer-machine synthesis near the end of §2.6.
- Front matter and historical/bibliographic material remain reading/reference context. They are not counted as artificial algorithm lessons.
- The answer appendix is not converted into a copied solution bank. Assessments use original data and questions.
- Numerical tables, notation index, and index remain source references. The course computes the quantities needed for its own examples.
- An implemented finite example or exhaustive small-state test is not a proof of a general theorem. The formal text identifies the proof assumptions and the limits of the example.

See [model scope](MODEL-SCOPE.md) for exact executable boundaries.
